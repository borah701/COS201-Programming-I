## Activity A: Input, Processing and Output (IPO)

1. **Area of a Rectangle**
   - Input: Length and width.
   - Processing: Multiply length by width.
   - Output: Area of the rectangle.

2. **Average of Four Scores**
   - Input: Four scores.
   - Processing: Add the scores and divide by four.
   - Output: Average score.

3. **Positive, Negative or Zero**
   - Input: A number.
   - Processing: Check whether it is greater than, less than or equal to zero.
   - Output: Positive, negative or zero.

4. **Total Cost of Items**
   - Input: Quantity (6) and unit price (₦250).
   - Processing: Multiply quantity by unit price.
   - Output: ₦1,500.
